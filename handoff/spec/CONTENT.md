## Notes on the copy

- The em-dash is banned across this project. Every dash in the copy above is a plain hyphen, deliberately. Keep it that way when you add anything.
- Arabic strings carry their own punctuation and numerals; render them as-is with `dir="rtl"` on `<html>`, and keep code, tags, periods and mono data blocks in `dir="ltr"` islands.
- Certificate names, issuers and URLs are never translated.
- The footer line differs per locale; the EN one credits Claude Code, the DE and AR ones do not. That is intentional, leave it.
- `avail` in EN has a trailing newline in the source. Trim it when you port.
